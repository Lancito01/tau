## Identity

- Idea: files orbit the cursor. An orrery is a model of orbiting planets.
- Wheel: round nodes on a thin ring over a dark disc, with a tether and a hub. No slices.
- Accent: violet #6D4AFF. Contrast check: 8 pairs, 0 AA failures.
- Icons: Segoe MDL2 Assets. The app icon is generated in `build.rs`.

## Logo brief

Symbol: a ring with one small node on it. It must read at 16px and at 1024px.
Deliver SVG, ICO and a 1200x630 social image. It must not use a citrus, a sliced
circle or orange.

## Voice

Plain, quick and exact. Say what happened, not how the app feels about it.
"Already as small as it can get", not "Oops! Nothing to compress".
